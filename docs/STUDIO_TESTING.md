# Studio test pass

CLI tests execute the actual economy, session, UI, audio, visibility, commerce, world, obby service, and server-entry code against explicit boundary fakes. They do not run Roblox's renderer, physics, native input/audio, Marketplace, or DataStores. The map verifier checks jump geometry mathematically; it does not establish how a jump feels. Record results from Studio and a private published Roblox test experience before public release.

## Opening, saving, and returning

1. Open `releases/MogMode.rbxl` in Roblox Studio on Windows or macOS. In an unpublished place or with Studio API access unavailable, confirm **Preview · saving off**, an explanation in Settings, no real purchases, disabled login claims, and no Output errors. Preview progress should reset when the server restarts.
2. Publish a separate private Roblox test experience and enable **Studio Access to API Services** in its Security settings. Leave `StudioUseLiveDataStores = true`. Reopen/play and confirm **Saved**, then **Auto-save pending** after playing and **Saved** after at most 30 seconds or pressing Settings → Save now. Do not point a Studio test at production data.
3. Earn Aura/XP, upgrade, adopt/train/equip pets, unlock zones, change settings/styles, claim a task/journal reward, and clear a course. Leave/rejoin and check every field. Rebirth keeps pets, zones, level/XP, journal, return progress, cosmetics, Flex Gems, and course history, but resets all Aura and power upgrades. Version-one profiles should migrate without dropping balances, receipts, or owned items.
4. The first saved visit starts a durable 24-hour wait. Confirm that changing the client clock, rejoining, or sending `ClaimLogin` cannot collect on day one. After 24 hours, claim once; check Aura + 40 XP, no Flex Gems, immediate persistence, no duplicate claim, and another full 24-hour wait. The seven rewards cycle; missing a day does not reset progress. Use isolated test data to advance server timestamps if testing the full cycle sooner.
5. With isolated test adapters or controlled service failures, exercise autosave retry, locked/expired sessions, overlapping joins, shutdown, and reconnect. Live profile failures must not silently use memory or overwrite stored data. Course finish/login/purchase rewards must appear only after a successful durable commit. Ordinary play remains subject to the autosave interval during a crash.

## Clicks, islands, and courses

6. Tap MOG, press F, and try controller X. Check a responsive cooldown fill and no more than five authoritative clicks per second, including very fast input. A cooldown caps rewards; it cannot identify every autoclicker. Check rejected clicks while dead, anchored for travel, loading, or saving. Test the 25-click pose in R6/R15 and restore joints after death, respawn, or disabling extra animation.
7. Unlock **all four destinations** in order: Brotein Boulevard (Lv 8 / 25,000 Aura), Rizz Reef (Lv 18 / 350,000), Sigma Summit (Lv 32 / 3,500,000), Final Boss Citadel (Lv 50 / 40,000,000). Test missing level, missing Aura, and missing previous island. A successful unlock should charge once and travel there; repeat visits should be free.
8. Travel both directions, return after streaming out, die during a trip, and repeat with slow streaming. The character should stay anchored during destination preparation and unfreeze afterward. Check spawn collision, island multipliers, next-objective changes, and recovery after falling. Other islands and their billboards/prompts must remain invisible, including instances retained by streaming.
9. Walk each island's 12-crystal circuit. Check the 8× click-value Aura payout, XP, per-player 45-second cooldown, distant/dead/wrong-world rejection, and prompt/visibility restoration after returning. Inspect floors while moving the camera low: grass, cliff, plaza, inset, and trim should not flicker over coplanar surfaces. Check lighting, the small remaining glow, and label distances in the actual renderer.
10. Play **all 15 courses**: each island has Warm-up Circuit (12 stages), Hop to the Top (22), and No-Cap Gauntlet (32). Start through a marker or Menu → Courses. Touch every numbered stage; skipping or finishing without the ordered route should give no reward. Check the narrower hard/expert landings and expert hazard strips in R6/R15. Confirm that every generated jump is comfortable enough to attempt and that no scenery blocks a landing.
11. Fall before and after checkpoints, touch hazards, die, use Retry checkpoint, switch course, and leave a course. Checkpoints are every four warm-up stages or eight hard/expert stages. They last for the current run and survive a character respawn, but not leaving the server or replacing the run. Deferred checkpoint touches during a save should continue the sequence correctly.
12. Check checkpoint Aura, first-clear Aura/XP, and the guaranteed exclusive pet on each hard/expert first clear. Warm-ups never give pets. Re-clearing cannot duplicate a pet. Each course and checkpoint has a saved five-minute reward cooldown; early repeats are explicitly practice and must not advance paid-out clear/daily counters. Validate first/repeat reward text and cooldown countdowns.
13. Force a course-finish save failure in a private test. No finish Aura or pet may be exposed; stepping off/back onto the finish after storage recovers must safely retry once. Leaving/rejoining must preserve successful clears, owned pets, and reward cooldowns. Compare real completion times and frustration across devices before tuning difficulty or progression.

## UI, audio, pets, and commerce

14. Test 320×568 and 390×844 portrait phones, landscape phones, tablet, desktop, and gamepad selection. The persistent HUD should remain compact. Follow the next-goal card, open/close the menu and all seven panels, change every zone/shop/task tab, scroll, and confirm/cancel an action. Check notches/insets, long names, readable contrast, touch targets, and separation from Roblox movement/jump controls. Headless geometry tests do not establish actual pixels or physical input.
15. Hear click, panel, crystal, error, upgrade, level, checkpoint, reset, finish, travel, mog, and purchase cues. Listen during fast clicking and overlapping effects; check for missing bundled assets, clipping, or harsh volume. Toggle SFX and extra animation independently. Ensure delayed notes do not play after muting and effects do not obscure course landings.
16. Adopt, train, equip/rest, and preview pets from every zone. Exclusive pets should direct unowned players to their course instead of offering an Aura purchase. Three slots are free; five require the verified ExtraPets pass. Check follower spacing, level-five stars, level-ten crowns, other players, travel, and respawn.
17. Verify tasks reset at midnight UTC; journal stamps are permanent; claims cannot repeat. Confirm saved reward cooldowns and login progress survive rebirth. Inspect boost timers after rejoin/expiry; timers run offline, repeat purchases add duration, and effects stop when expired.
18. Create actual Developer Products and Game Passes in Creator Dashboard, configure IDs in `src/server/ServerSettings.luau`, and test in a private published server. Zero IDs must remain unavailable. Check all ten products and three passes: correct listed/native price, exact quantity, cancellation, full-balance deferral, purchase-time pass refresh, receipt retry/deduplication, and eventual delivery to reconnecting players. Aura/XP boosts last 15 minutes, Aura boosts apply to clicks/crystals, and gameplay must never award Flex Gems. Do not prune receipt IDs or change the meaning of sold mappings.
19. Run a multiplayer server. Currencies, pets, crystal cooldowns, active courses, login claims, and saved progress must remain independent. Check frame time/memory across repeated visits, joins/leaves, and filled pet parties. Start with a 12-player limit, then tune from measured engine behavior.

Record actual devices, client versions, player counts, test results, and fixes. Human playtests are required to assess progression, difficulty, readability, and enjoyment.
